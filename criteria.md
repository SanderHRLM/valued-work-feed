A posting qualifies when both of these hold:

1. It is a real, currently open vacancy at the listed employer (or a brand of the same group that is active in
   the gift card, voucher, prepaid, incentive, loyalty, rewards, recognition or employee benefits business).
2. One of:
   - The employer's core business is gift cards, incentives, loyalty, rewards, recognition or employee
     benefits. Then professional roles in any function qualify (sales, partnerships, account management,
     marketing, product, customer success, operations, finance, legal, engineering, internships).
   - The employer is a large diversified group (travel, payments, retail, HR software and so on). Then only
     roles whose work is clearly about gift cards, prepaid, loyalty, incentives or rewards qualify.

Do not include:
- Open or speculative applications, talent pools and general "join us" pages.
- Warehouse, driver, cleaning, security and in-store retail roles.
- Roles located outside the region the employer is listed for, when the employer is a local branch of a
  global group (for example: for "Edenred Deutschland" only roles in Germany).
- Postings that are closed, expired or no longer accept applications.
