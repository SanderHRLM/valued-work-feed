A posting qualifies when all three of these hold:

1. It is a real, currently open vacancy at the listed employer (or a brand of the same group that is active in
   the gift card, voucher, prepaid, incentive, loyalty, rewards, recognition or employee benefits business).
2. Employer fit, one of:
   - The employer's core business is gift cards, incentives, loyalty, rewards, recognition or employee
     benefits.
   - The employer is a large diversified group (travel, payments, retail, HR software and so on). Then only
     roles whose work is clearly about gift cards, prepaid, loyalty, incentives or rewards qualify.
3. The role is in one of the wanted functions below.

Function priority (at most two postings per employer are published, so order matters):

1. First choice: commercial roles (sales, business development, account and key account management,
   partnerships, merchant or client relations, marketing), operational roles (operations, programme and
   project management, implementation, fulfilment and supply management) and executive or leadership roles
   (C-level, managing director, country manager, VP, director, head of).
2. Fallback, only when the employer has nothing from the first group: customer service, customer support and
   customer success roles.

Do not include:
- Software development, engineering, data science, data engineering, DevOps, QA, IT administration and other
  technical roles.
- Other support functions: finance, accounting, controlling, legal, compliance, HR, recruitment, design,
  office management.
- Internships, working-student and trainee positions.
- Open or speculative applications, talent pools and general "join us" pages.
- Warehouse, driver, cleaning, security and in-store retail roles.
- Roles located outside the region the employer is listed for, when the employer is a local branch of a
  global group (for example: for "Edenred Deutschland" only roles in Germany).
- Postings that are closed, expired or no longer accept applications.
