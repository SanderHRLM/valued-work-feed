⚙️ The Payroll squad

You'll join our Payroll squad, building Payflip's own payroll engine: a greenfield backend service that connects the Payflip platform to the world of Belgian payroll. That means:

  • Designing robust APIs and data models for payroll concepts like workers, absences and payslips.

  • Building reliable integrations with external payroll systems — asynchronous processing, queues and idempotent syncing.

  • Running part of our stack on Windows Server VMs in Azure (SQL Server, IIS, Remote Desktop), fully provisioned with Terraform.

  • Working in a domain where correctness and auditability matter: it's people's salaries.

  • Contributing to frontend features when needed, working with the squad to deliver payroll functionality end to end.

💬 Our hiring process for engineers

We aim to hire the best of the best at Payflip, and throughout the process we want to see candidates bring their best selves to the table. We'll dive into your previous track record and the expertise you're eager to bring, but also collaborate together with the product team to simulate the real job as close as possible.

After a quick informal chat with the CTO our interview is composed of 4 rounds:

  1. Screening call with two people from the product team (30-45 min, video call)

  2. A pair programming session with two engineers, where you are in the driver seat. You'll work in the Payflip codebase, on a real problem not solving a shady algorithm problem. (1h, video call + remote coding) The exercise includes frontend work, reflecting the fullstack nature of the role. We welcome backend-focused profiles: we don’t expect frontend mastery or a perfect solution, but we do expect you to navigate the code, reason through changes and make progress with us.

  3. You'll be presented a product + architectural problem and we'll ask you to write a short proposal for a solution. This is the only in person interview and you'll present this to the team in the Brussels office. We immediately couple the next interview to this so you only have to visit the office once

  4. In a final stage we'll be going into a behavioral interview where (together with a founder) we'll discuss your leadership vision, collaboration style and ambitions for the job at Payflip (45m, office)
