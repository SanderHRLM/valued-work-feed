Prezzee is a global leader in digital gifting and payments, having sold over 140 million gift cards through partnerships with more than 1,500 brands and serving over 6 million customers worldwide. Our mission is simple but powerful: to change lives through the power of giving — leveraging borderless payment technology and innovative digital solutions to help people connect and create meaningful moments.

About the role: 

We’re looking for a Senior Marketing Manager to own Prezzee’s global campaign calendar, bringing together Marketing, Product, Sales, and Partnerships to deliver standout B2C campaigns while driving consistency across B2B activity in our regions.

From global seasonal moments and product launches to promotions and strategic partnerships, you’ll lead end-to-end campaign planning and delivery that drives customer engagement, commercial growth, and memorable gifting experiences.

You’ll be at the centre of connecting teams, priorities, and markets to deliver impactful campaigns that support Prezzee’s growth ambitions.

What you’ll do

  • Own and manage Prezzee’s integrated global campaign calendar across B2C and B2B activity.
  • Lead end-to-end delivery of high-impact B2C campaigns across multiple channels.
  • Coordinate global campaign planning while partnering with regional teams on B2B activity.
  • Translate commercial objectives, product priorities, and business goals into coordinated campaign plans.
  • Deliver go-to-market plans for new products, features, and strategic initiatives.
  • Collaborate with Product, Sales, Partnerships, and Marketing teams to deliver commercial outcomes.
  • Lead campaign measurement, optimization, and post-campaign insights to improve future performance.
  • Coach and support a Campaign Executive while driving best practices across campaign operations.
  • Continuously improve campaign processes, workflows, and ways of working to support scale and efficiency.

What you’ll bring

  • 7+ years of experience delivering integrated marketing campaigns across multiple markets.
  • Experience leading cross-channel campaigns across CRM/email, paid media, social, SEO, PR, and web.
  • Strong stakeholder management and project leadership skills, with the ability to influence teams across functions and regions.
  • Commercial mindset with experience using data and insights to optimise campaign performance.
  • Experience working across cross-functional and global teams, balancing strategic thinking with hands-on delivery.
  • A proactive, organised, and collaborative approach with strong ownership and accountability.
  • The ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
