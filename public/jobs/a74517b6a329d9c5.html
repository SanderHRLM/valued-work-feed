Years of Experience Required

4+ years of overall professional experience as a BA, PO, or of similar job experience

Basic Job Description

Review, analyze and evaluate MI's Recognition, Incentive and Engagement web portals. Use creativity and strategy to meet client/product needs and document requirements, define objectives, and formulate solutions to support overall business strategies (75% of role). Rely on experience in working with clients, partners, and internal teams to drive strategic corporate initiatives and help guide software development projects from initiation to deployment, including front-end testing of the designed solution (25% of role).

Essential Duties and Responsibilities

  • Work with internal teams to prioritize and define modifications to MI's multi-tenant Incentive web application
  • Guide the design of MI's multi-tenant applications
  • Make decisions off market research and data insight to define the future vision of our applications
  • Strategize with clients on their web application change requests and bridge the gap between the client and internal web developers
  • Oversee the stages of a product's lifecycle from design through development
  • Ability to understand the customer's particular program and technical needs to position and document a solution mix which best addresses their needs
  • Participate in account strategy sessions with the intent of developing system recommendations
  • Work closely with other BAs, Web Developers, and Product Owners to formulate a product's launch or solution change
  • Tackle challenges and new findings in the midst of a sprint
  • Assist in front-end web testing of your designed solution
  • Support responses and solutions to client RFIs/RFPs
  • Ability to demonstrate and communicate the value of the product solution, not just document the functional requirements
  • Be resourceful and creative, consider how existing solutions might be combined to engender new sales opportunities
  • Support MI-specific value proposition hypotheses that clearly articulates 'why MI?' and 'why now?'
  • Present solution roadmap information to customers and prospects

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities Required

  • Builds a strong working relationship with both the prospect and the MI teams
  • Portrays communication skills to meet business needs through phone conversations, written communication, and face to face meetings

  • Use problem solving to work through and communicate roadblocks
  • Leads team meetings on a designed solution
  • Deliver informative, well-organized presentations based on business needs
  • Thoroughly understands MI's products to participate in prospect demos
  • Take input from others and be able to accurately apply it to the solution
  • Consistently delivers high-quality services to our clients
  • Be fluent in using web sites across many browsers/devices

  • Possesses understanding in the areas of application programming, database and system design (preferred, not required)
  • Ensure multi-platform and multi-device (PC, mobile & tablet) UI/UX responsive design
  • Exhibit confidence and an extensive knowledge of emerging industry practices, when solving business problems
  • Push creative thinking beyond the boundaries of existing industry practices and client mindsets
