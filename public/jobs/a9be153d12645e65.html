At Giftogram, we believe every thank-you should feel personal — not transactional. Our platform helps businesses appreciate employees, customers, and partners through meaningful, flexible gift cards that create real moments of recognition.

We're the #1 rated rewards and incentives platform on G2, used by more than 22,000 businesses — including 1 in 3 Fortune 500 companies. We're growing, and we're building the team to match.

The Role

As a Strategic Account Manager, you'll own a portfolio of Giftogram's most important customers — mid-market and enterprise organizations who already see Giftogram as a core part of how they recognize and reward people. Your job is to deepen those relationships, uncover expansion opportunities, and make sure every customer feels like they have a real partner in us.

This isn't a cold-start role. You're inheriting real accounts with real momentum. Your job is to grow them.

You'll balance strategy and execution: part trusted advisor, part growth driver, part internal advocate. The best people in this role don't just manage accounts — they make customers want to expand.

What You'll Own

  • A portfolio of active accounts, with full ownership of retention, growth, and satisfaction
  • Upsell and cross-sell opportunities across new teams, departments, and use cases
  • Multi-threaded relationships — you're never relying on a single point of contact
  • Structured business reviews and ROI conversations that connect Giftogram's value to what customers are actually trying to accomplish
  • Accurate pipeline forecasting and account planning in HubSpot
  • A voice for your customers internally — their wins are your wins

You Might Be a Great Fit If You Have

  • 3–5+ years in account management, strategic sales, or customer success — with a real track record of expansion, not just renewal
  • Experience working with HR, Finance, or Operations buyers at large organizations — you understand how enterprise decisions get made
  • The ability to build credibility quickly at multiple levels, from daily users to senior stakeholders
  • A consultative approach — you ask good questions, listen well, and bring ideas, not just check-ins
  • CRM discipline (we use HubSpot) and the organizational habits to manage a book without things falling through the cracks
