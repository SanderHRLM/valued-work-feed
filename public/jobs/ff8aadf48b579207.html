YOUR ROLE

As part of our fast-growing B2B2C business, we are looking for an entrepreneurial and commercially driven Partnerships & Growth Manager to lead the development of our advertising and partner ecosystem.

This role will be responsible for driving advertising revenue, traffic growth, and strategic partnerships while also helping shape Edenred's advertising products and monetization offerings. As an early member of the team, you will have the opportunity to build new commercial opportunities from the ground up and directly contribute to the growth of our digital business.

This is a builder role. We are looking for someone who enjoys creating new revenue streams, developing scalable partnership models, and turning digital assets into sustainable growth opportunities.

 

 

The Partnerships & Growth Manager is responsible for:

Partnership Development & Revenue Growth

  • Develop and expand strategic partnerships with advertisers, agencies, affiliate networks, media partners, platforms, and ecosystem partners.
  • Identify and secure new business opportunities that drive advertising revenue and user traffic.
  • Build and manage a strong pipeline of potential partners and advertisers.
  • Lead commercial negotiations and partnership agreements to maximize business impact.
  • Establish long-term relationships with key partners and continuously identify opportunities for collaboration and growth.

Affiliate & Traffic Growth Management

  • Develop and execute affiliate partnership strategies to drive qualified traffic, user acquisition, and transaction growth.
  • Recruit, onboard, and manage affiliate partners, publishers, loyalty platforms, content partners, influencers, and performance marketing networks.
  • Monitor and optimize affiliate performance against traffic, conversion, and revenue targets.
  • Continuously evaluate market trends and identify new acquisition channels and growth opportunities.

Advertising Product Development & Commercial Planning

  • Develop advertising products, sponsorship opportunities, and monetization solutions within Edenred's digital ecosystem.
  • Develop advertising packages, rate cards, and partnership proposals that align with market demand and business objectives.
  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders to define go-to-market strategies for new advertising offerings.
  • Continuously optimize advertising products based on partner feedback, performance results, and market trends.
  • Support the development of scalable monetization models that enhance both user experience and commercial value.

Performance Management & Business Analytics

  • Own and deliver key business KPIs, including advertising revenue, partnership revenue, traffic growth and campaign performance.
  • Build business cases and performance analyses to support strategic decision-making.
  • Prepare regular business reviews, forecasts, and management reports.
  • Track competitor activities, industry developments, and emerging digital advertising trends.

 

YOUR PROFILE

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Advertising, Communications, Digital Media, or related fields.
  • 5+ years of experience in digital advertising, media sales, affiliate marketing, business development, digital platforms, or related fields.
  • Proven track record of managing revenue-related and/or growth-related KPIs.
  • Experience working with advertisers, media agencies, affiliate networks, ad-tech platforms, or e-commerce ecosystems.
  • Solid understanding of digital advertising, affiliate marketing and platform monetization models.
  • Experience developing advertising solutions, commercial packages, media products, or monetization initiatives is highly preferred.
  • Strong business acumen with the ability to identify market opportunities and transform them into scalable commercial offerings.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a data-driven mindset.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation, and stakeholder management capabilities.
  • Ability to balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution.
  • Professional proficiency in Mandarin and English.
