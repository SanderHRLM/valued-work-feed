As Implementation Specialist, you will own the technical and operational onboarding of new North American clients, taking each one from signed contract to a live, well-configured platform. You are the bridge between the client's HR and IT teams, our Customer Success Managers, and our Product and Engineering teams. A high-quality and smooth on-time launch is your scorecard.

What you will do

  • Lead end-to-end platform implementations for new clients: kickoff, requirements gathering, configuration, testing, and go-live
  • Manage employee data onboarding: validate HRIS files, map fields, resolve data errors, and set up recurring data feeds
  • Set up and test API-based integrations: configure endpoints and authentication, make API calls (using tools like Postman) to fetch and validate data, read JSON responses, and troubleshoot failed calls or mismatched results
  • Configure the platform to each client's program design: recognition programs, award and badge catalogs, budgets, approval workflows, service milestones, and branding
  • Coordinate HRIS and SSO integrations (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Azure AD/Microsoft Entra, Okta) with client IT teams and our Engineering team
  • Partner with the Customer Success Manager to track milestones, and flag risks early
  • Run User Acceptance Testing with clients and drive issues to closure
  • Work with Design team on platform training material such as manuals, walkthrough guides
  • Hand off live accounts to the Customer Success team with clear documentation
  • Feed client requirements and recurring issues back to Product to improve the onboarding experience

What you bring

  • 5 to 8 years in SaaS implementation, technical onboarding, or solutions consulting, ideally with HR Tech or B2B platforms
  • Comfort with HRIS data: CSV and Excel data handling, field mapping, data validation, and troubleshooting
  • Working knowledge of SSO (SAML, OAuth), SFTP, and APIs; you do not need to code, but you can speak with IT teams confidently
  • Ability to analyze data, work with different excel sheets, read between the lines.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with experience working directly with North American clients
  • Detail orientation; you catch the issue before the client does
  • Willingness to work a shift with partial overlap with North American business hours

Nice to have

  • Experience with Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, or similar HCM platforms
  • Familiarity with Jira and Confluence, or similar tools
  • Prior exposure to employee engagement or R&R platforms
  • Prior experience working with the North America persona as service expectations are different that India persona

How we measure success

  • Implementations delivered on or ahead of the agreed go-live date
  • Client admin satisfaction/ NPS scores at handoff
  • Clean data loads and zero critical issues at launch
  • Launch success

Location and hours

Guwahati, India. Shift: 4 pm – 1 am IST.
