Objective of this role

We are seeking an experienced Catalogue Creator/Manager to oversee the development, management, and optimization of our product catalog. This role involves curating and managing a diverse range of products for corporate gifting, rewards, and loyalty programs. The ideal candidate will have over six years of experience in catalogue management, gift management, and a deep understanding of loyalty programs. This position requires a strategic thinker with exceptional organizational skills and the ability to drive innovation in product cataloguing.

Key Responsibilities

1. Catalogue Development and Management

  • Develop and maintain a comprehensive and user-friendly product catalog.
  • Regularly update the catalog with new products, ensuring accuracy and completeness.
  • Implement best practices for catalog design and presentation to enhance user experience.

2. Corporate Gifting and Rewards

  • Manage and curate a selection of products for corporate gifting and rewards programs.
  • Work with vendors and suppliers to source high-quality products that meet corporate gifting standards.
  • Develop and implement strategies to optimize the impact of corporate gifting initiatives.

3. Loyalty Program Integration

  • Utilize knowledge of loyalty programs to integrate catalog products effectively.
  • Collaborate with the marketing and loyalty teams to ensure catalog offerings align with loyalty program objectives.
  • Analyse and report on the performance of catalog products within loyalty programs.

4. Gift Management

  • Oversee the procurement, storage, and distribution of gift items.
  • Develop processes for tracking and managing inventory levels.
  • Ensure timely and accurate delivery of gifts to clients and employees.

5. Vendor and Supplier Relations

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with vendors and suppliers.
  • Negotiate terms and manage contracts to achieve favourable pricing and quality.
  • Monitor vendor performance and address any issues promptly.

6. Market Research and Analysis

  • Stay updated on industry trends and competitor catalog offerings.
  • Conduct market research to identify new product opportunities and customer preferences.
  • Use insights to continuously improve and innovate the catalog.

Skills

  • Expertise in catalogue creation and management tools and software.
  • Strong understanding of loyalty programs and their impact on product cataloguing.
  • Excellent organizational and project management skills.
  • Strong analytical abilities and attention to detail.
  • Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively across departments and with external partners.

Qualification

  • Experience: Minimum of 6 years of experience in catalogue management, corporate gifting, rewards, and gift management. Proven experience with loyalty programs and integrating catalog products within such frameworks.
  • Education: Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, or a related field. Relevant certifications or additional qualifications are a plus.
