Humand is an HR tech SaaS platform helping companies build stronger internal cultures through employee engagement, communication, and development tools. We operate in 50+ countries with over 2M active users and recently closed the largest Series A in Latin American history.

We're looking for a Head of Marketing / CMO to lead a 50+ person global marketing team and own the revenue contribution of marketing worldwide.

The core of this role is taking the marketing team to the next level. The next stage is to keep that pace while raising the level of the operation: mature internal processes, a reporting system leadership can trust, clear ownership across areas, and budget decisions grounded in solid data. The goal isn't only to scale faster, it's to scale efficiently.

You'll report directly to the CEO, sit at the leadership table alongside Sales, Product, and Finance, and own marketing's number in front of the board and investors.

What You'll Own

  • Revenue Accountability & Measurement: own marketing's contribution to pipeline and MRR across LATAM, Brazil, EMEA, and North America, including the multi-touch attribution model, the full-funnel measurement framework, and board and investor reporting.
  • Strategy & Planning: own the global marketing strategy and its translation into regional plans across four regions and 50+ countries, build the projection model connecting investment to output, and own global budget allocation.
  • Organization & Leadership: lead, coach, and develop the marketing org (58 people across 11 sub-areas), design the next layer of the structure, finish maturing the matrix model (global specialists + regional teams), build the OKR and KPI system per vertical, and define levels, salary bands, and career paths.
  • AI-first operator: set the criteria for where AI belongs and where it doesn't, and redesign processes and roles around it. The mandate is to grow output and efficiency without defaulting to more headcount.
  • Cross-functional: partner with the Head of Sales on a joint MKT–Sales roadmap, work with Finance on budget, forecasting, and CAC/LTV discipline, and set the positioning and product marketing strategy together with Product and the CTO.

What We're Looking For

  • Many years in B2B SaaS marketing, leading a marketing org of 40+ people across multiple regions.
  • Experience leading a multi-vertical marketing organization (Growth, Paid Media, Product Marketing, Data & Analytics, Design, Multimedia, Web, Brand & Content).
  • Proven experience operating in HISPAM, Brazil, EMEA, and North America, including leading expansion into at least one of them from outside the region.
  • Proven experience directing the build of a multi-touch attribution model in B2B SaaS.
  • Experience building a financial projection model for marketing and running board and investor reporting for marketing.
  • Experience designing and running a matrix marketing organization and building the people infrastructure of a marketing org.
  • Demonstrated ability to identify and develop leaders, including promoting specialists into roles that span disciplines beyond their own.
  • AI-first mindset at the process level, not the tool level. You've restructured workflows, roles, or teams around AI and can show the efficiency it produced.
  • A record of professionalizing a marketing operation.
  • Deep fluency in HubSpot, BI/data warehouse environments, GA4, and the paid media ecosystem (Google, LinkedIn, Meta).
  • Fluent English, written and spoken, plus Spanish and/or Portuguese.

Nice to have

  • Experience scaling into APAC.
  • Experience in HR tech, or selling to HR / People / Internal Communications buyers.
  • Experience with PLG or hybrid PLG + sales-led motions.
