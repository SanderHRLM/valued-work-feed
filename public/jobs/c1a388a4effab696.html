We're looking for a driven and strategic Sales Manager to spearhead our growth engine. You will own the full sales funnel, from prospecting and lead generation to closing enterprise deals and managing accounts. This is a high-impact role at a company scaling fast, and you'll have a front-row seat to shaping how we grow.

What you'll do

  • Establish sales opportunities through networking, relationship building, strategic outreach, and prospecting
  • Build and own a corporate prospect database; manage the entire sales funnel from lead generation to deal closure and ongoing account management
  • Identify key decision makers through targeted research and prospecting tools
  • Achieve and exceed sales targets through acquisition of new clients and by growing revenue from existing accounts
  • Target a specific set of customers and find creative ways to reach decision makers and close deals
  • Drive strategic partnerships that contribute to lead generation and accelerate deal closures

What we're looking for

  • Master's Degree in Marketing, Business Administration, or a related field
  • 3+ years of experience in a corporate sales role, preferably in SaaS (high order value), banking sales, media sales, or hospitality sales
  • Proven track record in Corporate Sales, Account Management, and outbound outreach
  • Exceptional communication skills, both written and verbal, along with strong presentation abilities
  • Strong client relationship management skills; you know how to build trust and long-term partnerships
  • Proficiency in MS Office and CRM tools (Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar)

Required skills

  • Strategic thinking
  • Selling skills
  • Relationship building
  • Communication skills
