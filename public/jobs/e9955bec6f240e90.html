The role

As a Program Operations Associate on our North America Customer Experience (NA-CX) team, you will keep our clients' recognition programs running smoothly every day. You will handle platform operations, data, reporting, and engagement campaigns behind the scenes, so our Customer Success Managers (CSMs) can focus on strategy and growth. You will work closely with CSMs, Implementation, Support, Solutions, and Product teams to carve solutions and address client asks.

What you will do

  • Run day-to-day program operations for a portfolio of North American accounts: user data updates, budget de-allocations and allocations, award approvals, service milestone runs, and platform configuration changes
  • Monitor recurring HRIS data feeds and batch jobs; investigate and resolve file errors before they reach the client
  • Build client-ready reports and dashboards on platform adoption, recognition activity, and redemptions; prepare data and insights for Quarterly Business Reviews
  • Plan and execute engagement campaigns (seasonal and themed recognition campaigns, new feature launches): client workbooks, badges, communication templates, and wrap-up reports
  • Draft client communications, admin guides, and FAQs
  • Triage client requests and tickets, resolve what you can, and route the rest with clear context
  • Track action items, renewals data, and account health signals for CSMs
  • Identify repetitive tasks and help automate them with tools like Power Automate, Excel, or AI assistants
  • Understand new features being built onto platform, how to activate any and demo them to the admins

What you bring

  • 3 to 5 years in customer operations, program operations, client servicing, or a similar role, ideally at VC or a SaaS company
  • Strong Excel or Google Sheets skills: pivots, lookups, data cleanup, and basic charting
  • Clear, professional written English for client-facing emails and documents
  • Organized and reliable: you can context-switch easily, juggle many small tasks across accounts without dropping any
  • Curiosity about data and a habit of asking "why" when numbers look off
  • Willingness to work a shift with partial overlap with North American business hours

Nice to have

  • Experience with HR Tech, employee engagement, or R&R platforms
  • Exposure to Jira, Freshdesk or a CRM
  • Comfort using AI tools to speed up reporting and content creation

How we measure success

  • Operational requests completed accurately and within SLA
  • Data feed and batch issues caught and fixed before client escalation
  • On-time delivery of reports, QBR data, and campaign materials
  • CSM and client feedback on quality and responsiveness

Location and hours

Guwahati, India. Shift: 4 pm – 1 am IST
