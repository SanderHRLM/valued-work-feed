Monizze is on a mission to help employers support the well-being and engagement of their people.
From our roots in electronic meal, eco, and gift vouchers, we’ve evolved into a comprehensive Reward Management System, offering smart, flexible, and human-centered salary solutions.

As part of this transformation, we’ve launched Reward Flex, a new platform that allows employees to shape their own benefits package with total freedom. Combined with our Social Vouchers offering, we’re bringing a new kind of value to thousands of workers across Belgium.

To support this growth, we’re looking for a Sales Executive with strong business development skills to promote and sell both Social Vouchers and Reward Flex solutions. This role requires strong consultative selling skills, the ability to understand complex client needs, and to translate them into tailored combinations of products.

The role will primarily focus on our 500–999 beneficiaries segment, involving high-level discussions with C-level decision-makers. Success in this position requires the ability to build trusted relationships with senior stakeholders and leverage a strong professional network across Belgium.
