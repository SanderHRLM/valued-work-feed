The Local Operations Manager is responsible for overseeing and coordinating local payment and means-of-payment operational activities within a country or legal entity. The role ensures efficient execution, operational continuity, service quality, compliance, and operational governance across activities related to digital cards, plastic cards, voucher operations, production coordination, distribution flows, reconciliation support, operational controls, and payment operational services.
