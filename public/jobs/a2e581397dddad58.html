We are looking for a commercially focused Strategic Account Manager to own a portfolio of key Australian customers. You will be the primary commercial relationship owner, accountable for customer retention, portfolio performance, strategic growth, renewals and senior stakeholder engagement.

You will develop a clear view of each customer's business, priorities, risks and growth potential. You will turn that understanding into practical account plans, executive engagement, qualified opportunities and coordinated action across EML.

This role suits someone who combines sound commercial judgement with disciplined execution. You will bring recommendations, not only issues, and lead customer outcomes across EML without taking ownership away from specialist operational and technical teams.

What you will do

  • Own the relationship for a portfolio of strategic customers, accountable for retention, revenue and long-term growth
  • Build and deliver account plans that set clear priorities, map key stakeholders and anticipate renewals
  • Spot risks early and coordinate the right support across EML before they become renewal issues
  • Develop trusted relationships from operational contacts to executive decision-makers, positioning EML as a strategic partner
  • Grow existing accounts by identifying and converting upsell, cross-sell and product adoption opportunities
  • Lead renewals, pricing discussions and commercial negotiations, protecting both the relationship and margin
  • Run QBRs and executive reviews that end in clear decisions and next steps
  • Track portfolio performance (revenue, margin, usage and pipeline) and keep CRM and reporting accurate
  • Be the voice of the customer across Product, Operations, Finance, Risk and Compliance, and own the commercial side of escalations
  • Contribute to team initiatives that improve how we work and engage customers

What you will bring

  • Experience in strategic or key account management, customer success or a similar B2B commercial role
  • Experience in payments, fintech, card issuing or another regulated environment
  • A track record of retaining and growing complex, high-profile customers, including renewals and commercial negotiations
  • Strong commercial judgement: you weigh revenue, margin, risk and delivery capacity to recommend the right course of action
  • Confidence building trusted relationships and influencing stakeholders up to executive level, both with customers and internally
  • An analytical approach, using revenue, usage and pipeline data to spot trends, risks and opportunities
  • Clear, concise communication, from account updates to executive briefs
  • A collaborative, ownership mindset, working across Product, Operations, Finance, Risk and Compliance to deliver outcomes
  • Adaptability in a fast-moving payments and regulatory environment

  • Participation in the company bonus scheme
  • Flexible working - Be empowered to work smarter, in a way that suits your lifestyle
  • 12w Parental Leave, Birthday Leave, Volunteer leave & Paid Professional memberships.
