Summary

Augeo is seeking a Sr. Event Manager, Incentive Travel to lead corporate incentive travel programs, recognition experiences, President's Club trips, and other high-impact events for corporate clients. In this role, you'll serve as the primary client contact, overseeing programs from strategy and planning through onsite execution and final reconciliation.

You'll partner with clients, suppliers, destinations, hotels, and internal teams to deliver exceptional participant experiences while maintaining a relentless focus on client satisfaction, operational excellence, and business results.

This role is ideal for someone who combines strong incentive travel expertise with consultative client leadership, strategic thinking, financial acumen, and a passion for creating memorable experiences.

What You'll Do

Project Leadership & Client Partnership

  • Lead mid-to-large-scale incentive travel programs and events from kickoff through execution.
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for clients, building trust through proactive communication and reliable delivery.
  • Facilitate client calls, present recommendations, and guide strategic planning conversations.
  • Manage project timelines, budgets, financial trackers, and internal workflows using agency tools and systems.
  • Lead internal planning meetings and coordinate cross-functional teams to ensure successful delivery.
  • Consult with clients on program strategy, participant experience, destination recommendations, and event design.

Incentive Travel Planning & Execution

  • Design and execute all aspects of incentive travel programs, including destination selection, hotel and venue sourcing, room blocks, food & beverage, transportation, excursions, group activities, gifting, registration, and attendee communications.
  • Partner with hotels, destination management companies (DMCs), transportation providers, and other suppliers to create exceptional participant experiences.
  • Oversee supplier negotiations, contracting, and ongoing vendor management.
  • Manage attendee experience from pre-program communications through onsite execution.
  • Conduct site visits and travel for onsite program management and execution (approximately 25-30%).
  • Ensure all program elements are delivered seamlessly while maintaining the highest standards of service and client satisfaction.

Financial & Operational Management

  • Develop and manage program budgets, forecasts, and financial reporting.
  • Monitor program expenses and identify opportunities to maximize value while maintaining quality.
  • Lead post-program reconciliation, billing, and financial closeout processes.
  • Ensure project deliverables, timelines, and client expectations are met throughout the program lifecycle.

Post-Event Wrap-Up

  • Lead internal and client debrief sessions.
  • Analyze participant feedback and program outcomes.
  • Identify best practices and process improvements to strengthen future programs.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement across the team and client portfolio.

What You Bring

  • 6-8 years of experience managing incentive travel programs, meetings, events, or related client-facing experiences.
  • Agency, third-party planning, incentive travel, meetings, or hospitality industry experience preferred.
  • Proven success leading complex programs with multiple stakeholders, suppliers, and moving parts.
  • Experience managing program budgets and financial reconciliation.
  • Strong supplier negotiation and contract management skills.
  • Exceptional organization, attention to detail, and project management capabilities.
  • Excellent communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Professional presence and confidence in client-facing environments.
  • Ability to adapt quickly, solve problems creatively, and lead with positivity.

You're a Fit If You Are…

  • A confident client leader who thrives on building trusted partnerships.
  • Passionate about incentive travel, recognition programs, and creating exceptional experiences.
  • Resourceful, proactive, and calm under pressure.
  • Highly organized and able to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Collaborative and entrepreneurial, with a client-first mindset.
  • Energized by turning complex programs into seamless experiences.
  • Excited to create memorable moments that motivate, reward, and inspire participants.
