WE WILL VIBE WITH YOU

We are looking for an Account Manager dedicated to partnerships as part of our Sales Team.


As an Account Manager, you will manage and develop our network of indirect partners. As the true orchestrator of the partner ecosystem, you will be responsible for revenue growth through our indirect channels and the development of new strategic opportunities. 


More specifically, you will be responsible for:


Management and development of the partner portfolio

  • Manage and optimize an existing portfolio of indirect partners
  • Develop sustainable strategic relationships with key partners
  • Identify and recruit new partners to expand our distribution network.
  • Design and implement commercial action plans tailored to each type of partner.

Commercial performance and review

  • Define and monitor sales objectives per channel and per partner
  • Review commercial performance data and identify growth drivers
  • Interpret market trends and project sales results
  • Produce regular reports and strategic presentations for management

Network animation and training

  • Train partners on our solutions, products and services
  • Organize partner events (webinars, workshops, conventions)
  • Provide continuous commercial support and field assistance
  • Develop tools and materials to facilitate indirect sales

Cross-functional collaboration

  • Work closely with Marketing teams to develop partner campaigns
  • Coordinate with Communiction to create adapted content and materials.
  • Collaborate with IT to optimize partner tools and processes
  • Participate in cross-functional commercial development projects



WE WILL VIBE WITH YOU


  • You have proven experience of minimum 5 years in managing and developing a network of indirect partners, ideally in a B2B2C environment.
  • You are bilingual Dutch and French
  • You feel comfortable with digital tools, including Microsoft and CRM (Salesforce is a plus). You have a strong interest and ideally already used Artificial Intelligence in a professional context (data analysis, automation or commercial optimization,…).
  • You possess strong analytical skills, particularly within commercial environments, with a proven ability to interpret complex performance metrics, develop reliable forecasts, and synthesize data-driven insights into actionable strategic recommendations.

Personal qualities:

  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills with the ability to federate a partner network
  • Autonomy, proactivity and entrepreneurial spirit
  • Results-oriented with business acumen
  • Ability to work in project mode with cross-functional teams
  • Excellent communicator, comfortable in presentations
  • Curiosity for new technologies (including AI) and innovation
