The team you'll be joining

Our Payment Operations & Change team plays a key role in EML's transformation journey, modernising processes, driving automation, and creating a best-in-class operation. The team manages projects and operational initiatives while overseeing critical relationships with payment networks, card schemes (Visa, Mastercard), acquirers, processors, card bureaus, and associated service providers across Australia and globally.

The Role

We are looking for a hands-on Project Manager who thrives on delivery and takes pride in seeing initiatives through to completion. This role is critical to EML's global Operations team, delivering card scheme and tokenisation projects, payment operations transformation and cross-functional initiatives.

You will have subject matter knowledge of the Payments industry, be detail-oriented and have a track record of delivery. Success in this role means combining structured project management, including clear planning, risk management, governance, and clear accountability, with the agility to roll up your sleeves and drive actions forward.

You will be a safe pair of hands in the Payment Operations team, supporting both large and small initiatives. From individual client delivery projects, and associated reporting, to improving our card scheme project delivery for efficiency and scale.

This role demands exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills. You will provide clarity and confidence through concise, accurate reporting and updates.
