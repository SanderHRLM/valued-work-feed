The Business Development Lead will play a key role in bringing high-value financial services and loyalty partnerships to life for Virgin Australia Business Flyer (VABF).

Working alongside the Head of Business Development and Head of SME Loyalty, this role requires a commercially experienced relationship leader who can operate confidently with senior and C-suite stakeholders, progress complex opportunities and help deliver strategic products and partnerships to market.

In this role, you will work across an established pipeline as well as new opportunities, taking ownership of commercial discussions, negotiations, contracting and internal delivery to turn partnership opportunities into commercially sound outcomes.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and progress high-value strategic partnership opportunities across financial services, payments and loyalty, supporting Velocity's growth priorities.
  • Manage and advance a portfolio of commercial opportunities, maintaining momentum from senior partner engagement through to execution and launch.
  • Develop compelling partner propositions, commercial models and business cases that clearly articulate value for Velocity, VABF and prospective partners.
  • Own end-to-end commercial negotiations and contracting, including deal structuring, terms, commercial agreements and contract close, working closely with Legal, Finance and other specialist teams.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior internal and external stakeholders, confidently navigating complex corporate environments and aligning stakeholders around commercial outcomes.
  • Coordinate cross-functional delivery across areas such as Product, Marketing, Legal, Finance and Operations to move agreed partnerships and propositions through to market.
  • Represent Velocity with credibility and commercial confidence in senior external conversations and C-suite engagements, with the ability to independently lead meetings and progress discussions.

Please note: This is a 12 month opportunity with potential to extend.

Requirements

  • 7+ years' commercial, partnerships or business development experience, with strong experience in financial services and/or loyalty. Experience across payments, cards, banking or fintech will be highly regarded.
  • Proven experience managing complex strategic partnerships and commercial agreements, ideally within large corporate environments and involving significant commercial value.
  • Strong knowledge of financial services and loyalty ecosystems, with an understanding of how partners, products, payments and customer value propositions come together.
  • Demonstrated experience leading commercial negotiations and contract processes, including shaping deal structures, negotiating terms and working with Legal and internal stakeholders through to execution.
  • Strong commercial judgement with the ability to understand complex propositions, develop business cases and translate opportunities into clear commercial outcomes.
  • A track record of progressing opportunities from senior-level engagement and proposition development through negotiation, contracting, internal delivery and launch.
  • Exceptional stakeholder management and executive presence, with the confidence to engage, influence and independently lead conversations with C-suite executives, senior corporate partners and internal leaders.
  • Strong relationship-building capability within financial services and/or loyalty, with the ability to quickly establish credibility and trusted partnerships at senior levels.
  • Highly effective at working across cross-functional teams, bringing together Product, Marketing, Legal, Finance and operational stakeholders to keep complex commercial initiatives moving.
  • Commercially driven, resilient and comfortable operating at pace and with ambiguity, with the communication skills and ownership mindset to hit the ground running.
