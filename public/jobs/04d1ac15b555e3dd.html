What you do as a Performance Marketer

As a Performance Marketer, you’re the engine behind our (international) growth. In this role you manage, analyse and optimise performance marketing campaigns across digital channels. You work closely with the marketing and e-commerce team and play a key role in scaling our international platforms.

Who are you?

  • You have at least 2 years of relevant experience with performance channels (Google Ads is a must, Social/Display a big plus).
  • You’re analytically sharp and love seeing ROAS go through the roof.
  • You’re entrepreneurial, spot opportunities in the market and take charge to seize them.
  • You have an excellent command of Dutch and English.

Benefits of working with us
