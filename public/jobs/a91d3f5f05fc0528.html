Job Description


The Manager, Purchasing & Design is responsible for providing leadership/direction to a team of Purchasing & Design Specialists to ensure successful delivery of Travel and Events budgets, proposals and contracts. Develop and mentor the assigned team of Purchasing & Design Specialists to ensure their career path and growth.


Job Description Details:


  • Plan and monitor staffing and workload, ensuring appropriate resources are accountable for expected deliverables
  • Participate in the hiring process including hiring decisions
  • Mentor and train direct reports on department process compliance and position expectations
  • Provide performance feedback throughout year by conducting regular one-on-one meetings with direct reports
  • Oversee all project work on assigned workload
  • Ensure team members are actively participating in Client Playbooks to drive team organization and optimal performance
  • Drive operational excellence and growth in collaboration with Client Services
  • Attend proposal kick-off and turnover meetings
  • Conduct regular file reviews with Purchasing & Design Specialist until file is turned over to Event Management
  • Monitor proposal timeline management to ensure deliverable dates are met
  • Proof proposal budgets against program agenda
  • Assist with pricing standards to maximize overall profitability
  • Review/approve budgets and proformas
  • Proof and review client and supplier contracts with Purchasing & Design Specialist
  • Ensure compliance with negotiated savings documentation
  • Participate in client presentations
  • Develop and maintain industry relationships including hotels, NSOs, DMCs, and CVB
  • Perform other duties as assigned

Additional Job Description Details


Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience
  • Minimum ten years of extensive meeting/incentive & event management experience
  • Client and supplier contract expertise
  • 2-3 years’ experience in successfully delegating and leading individuals/team
  • High level proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, especially Word, PowerPoint and Excel
  • Proven ability in project management and financial aptitude
  • Demonstrated ability to operate effectively in a deadline driven environment
  • Strong editing and writing skills
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to handle multiple projects
  • Ability to work independently as well as a member of a team
  • Superior customer service ethic and high expectations for quality
  • Ability to travel as needed
