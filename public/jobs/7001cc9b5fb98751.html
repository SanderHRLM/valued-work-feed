About Reward360

Reward360 is a leading data-driven loyalty and rewards technology company, enabling enterprises to build impactful customer engagement programs. With a strong presence across India, Middle East, and Asia, Reward360 partners with global brands to deliver scalable loyalty platforms, analytics-led insights, and a rich rewards ecosystem.

Role Overview

We are looking for a Director – Business Development to lead enterprise client acquisition. This role is ideal for a high-performing leader who can scale revenue, expand markets, and build long-term partnerships in a B2B / enterprise environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own and execute business development and growth strategy aligned with company objectives
  • Drive enterprise sales, strategic alliances, and partner-led revenue growth
  • Identify new markets, client segments, and monetization opportunities
  • Build and manage a strong sales pipeline, ensuring predictable revenue
  • Lead commercial negotiations and close high-value B2B deals
  • Collaborate with Product, Marketing, Analytics, and Client Success teams
  • Track KPIs, revenue metrics, and growth performance
  • Represent Reward360 in industry forums and partner discussions

Candidate Profile

  • 10 to 14 years of experience in business development, enterprise sales, partnerships, or growth roles
  • Proven track record of closing large-value B2B deals
  • Strong experience in stakeholder management, negotiations, and strategic planning

Preferred Experience

  • Experience in SaaS, loyalty platforms, fintech, customer engagement, or digital ecosystems is preferred
