Your mission at Epassi

You own the B2B2C activation of our sports benefit across our existing customer base. You build and run the campaigns, toolkits, and automated onboarding journeys that turn low sports activation into measurable subscription growth - working hand in hand with Customer Success on every client-facing moment.

Your responsibilities will be:

  • Own the always-on sports activation programme end to end - from HR-facing campaign angles to the employee-facing assets that drive real usage
  • Build the toolkit Customer Success uses in every activation conversation, and own the kick-off process so CS never opens a client conversation without the right materials
  • Design, build, and maintain the lifecycle flows that carry new sports subscribers from sign-up to habit
  • Track activation and subscription metrics against the OKR and feed results back into campaign planning

You'll be successful in this role if you:

  • Drive a measurable increase in sports activation rates across low-activation customer segments
  • Hit the net new sports subscription quarterly target under the Retain and Expand OKR
  • Deliver a CS toolkit and kick-off process the team actually uses
  • Get onboarding automation running without manual intervention from marketing or CS

What you'll be doing (daily):

  • Build and maintain CSM toolkits: talking points, scripts, digital flyers, posters
  • Plan and run the campaign calendar, using moments like Mental Health Week and World Wellbeing Week as hooks
  • Own the client kick-off checklist for sports activation together with CS
  • Support the promo-op-locatie offer: branded materials, agency coordination, and the CS booking process
  • Maintain the shared activation tracker with CS and translate data into clear conversion insights
  • Design and build automated onboarding email flows for new sports subscribers
  • Work with Product and Ops to define trigger data (activation status, usage thresholds)
  • Test and iterate onboarding journeys based on activation and engagement data
  • Report activation rate, net new subscriptions, and campaign performance monthly into the MBR cycle
