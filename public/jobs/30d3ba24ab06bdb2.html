We are hiring VAS Field Officers on a 3-month contract. In this field role you will work directly with cardholders at company sites and employee residences.

Key Responsibilities

  • Distribute cards, and collect KYC of the authorized individuals, ensuring we comply with mandatory compliance regulations.
  • Onboard cardholders to our app, educate about the products and services along with the proper usage of the card.
  • Financial inclusion training for cardholders to onboard new users to the C3Pay app and assist cardholders with day-to-day issues related to their card or mobile app.
  • Remittance visits on salary days to assist cardholders to transfer money through C3Pay App.
  • Marketing campaigns for C3Pay product service awareness, award ceremonies at employee residences.
  • Conduct fraud awareness trainings at employee residence.
  • Corporate VAS Training presentation to Payroll and welfare team.

Required Skills & Profile

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance/Commerce.
  • Demonstrable experience as a field officer in any banking product.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills.
  • Multi language speaker.
  • Microsoft Office skills.
