We’re looking for someone early in their career who’s curious, confident, and ready to get into SaaS sales.

  • Use existing databases and tools to identify and connect with the right audience

  • Set up qualified meetings for the sales team
  • Experiment with different outreach approaches

Our Ideal Candidate

  • Graduate with 0–1 year experience (freshers with strong intent can apply)
  • Good communication skills (written + verbal)
  • Basic understanding of lead generation / sales outreach
  • Exposure to tools (LinkedIn, CRM, email tools, etc.) is a plus
  • Basic understanding of data handling
  • Proactive, confident, and willing to learn

Good to Have

  • MBA is a plus
  • Exposure to SaaS / B2B sales
  • Familiarity with data tools or databases

What You’ll Get

  • Hands-on exposure to real SaaS sales
  • Opportunity to learn outbound, prospecting, and client conversations
  • A fast-paced environment where you’ll learn by doing
  • Clear growth path into core sales roles
